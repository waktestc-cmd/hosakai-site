01 / Our perspective

A quiet dashboard is not proof of a quiet network.

Coverage is not the same as confidence. For SOC teams, detection engineers and security leaders, the gap between the two matters.

Breachmatrix is an AI-native, AI-first security company bringing together offensive security testing and defensive security automation. Testing runs within client-controlled platforms to help teams identify blind spots, assess their defences and strengthen the security of their environments.

03 / A little more context

Before you join.

A few things worth knowing.

What does AI-native mean here?

AI is central to how we build Breachmatrix, not an add-on. Our focus is AI-first automation for offensive and defensive security testing, helping teams identify gaps and improve their defences.

Where does security testing run?

Security testing runs within client-controlled platforms. Breachmatrix is built to help secure those environments while clients retain control of their platforms.

Who is Breachmatrix for?

SOC teams, detection engineers and security leaders who want to question their coverage, not just count their alerts.
